Simple Tomato Soup

A quick soup for cold days. It takes about 30 minutes and uses only a few things you likely have at home, so it is a good choice for a weekday evening when you are tired.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 800 g canned tomatoes
  • ½ tsp salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pot.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes.
    Stir often.
  3. Add the tomatoes. See our tips.
